Privacy Policy

Eyeglass Gallery ("we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, contact details, prescription and insurance information, and any messages you send us through our website forms or when booking an appointment.

How we use it

We use your information to provide eyecare and eyewear services, process orders and insurance claims, respond to your enquiries, and, where you have consented, send you updates. We do not sell your personal information.

Protecting your information

We take reasonable steps to safeguard your information. Health and prescription records are handled in accordance with applicable Ontario privacy legislation. We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here or as required by law.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw consent to marketing, by contacting us at info@eyeglassgallery.ca or 905.331.5999.

This is a general policy provided for the website. Please confirm the final wording with your own legal/privacy advisor before publishing.
