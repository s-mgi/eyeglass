Inside Our Optometry Clinic
01

In-House Edging Lab

Our computerized, automated edging system and in-house optical lab let us cut and fit lenses efficiently, decreasing turnaround so you get your eyewear sooner.

02

Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT)

Our Topcon Maestro 2 OCT captures high-resolution, cross-sectional images of the retina, non-invasively, to detect and monitor retinal disease within minutes.

03

RetinaLogik Visual Field Testing

Our RetinaLogik perimeter uses virtual reality and AI to map your full scope of vision and help detect glaucoma and other conditions, quickly and comfortably.

04

Digital Retinal Imaging

A digital photo of the back of your eye, showing the retina, optic disc and blood vessels, helps us pinpoint early signs of disease.
