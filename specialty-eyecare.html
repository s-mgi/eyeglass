From routine comprehensive exams to managing complex eye disease, we care for your whole-eye health with advanced on-site technology.

Exams for every stage of life

A thorough eye exam does more than update your prescription, it protects your long-term vision and eye health. We tailor each exam to the patient.

  • Children's eye exams
  • Adult & senior eye exams
  • Diabetic eye exams

Specialty Services
01

Dry Eye Therapy

Assessment and treatment for dry, gritty, watery or irritated eyes, with products and therapies to restore comfort.

02

Glaucoma Management

Early detection and ongoing monitoring of glaucoma to help protect the optic nerve and preserve your sight.

03

Macular Degeneration

Screening and co-management of age-related macular degeneration (AMD) using advanced retinal imaging.

04

Cataract Monitoring

Detection, monitoring and surgical co-management so you get the right care at the right time.

05

Digital Eye Strain

Relief for screen fatigue, with blue-light options and the simple 20-20-20 habit to rest your eyes.

06

Myopia Control

Options to help slow the progression of near-sightedness in children, including specialty contact lenses.
