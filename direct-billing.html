Direct Billing & Coverage

We make paying for your eyecare simple, with direct insurance billing and government coverage for eligible patients.

OHIP coverage

OHIP covers a comprehensive eye exam for many Ontario residents. You are generally eligible for an OHIP-insured exam if you are:

01

19 & Under

Children and youth aged 19 and under are covered for a routine eye exam once a year.

02

65 & Over

Seniors aged 65 and older are covered for a routine eye exam.

03

Medical Conditions

Patients with certain conditions (such as diabetes, glaucoma or cataracts) may be covered for medically necessary exams.

Insurance

Direct insurance billing

Where your plan allows, we bill your vision and health insurance directly, so you pay less out of pocket and skip the paperwork. We work with many of Canada's major benefits providers.
